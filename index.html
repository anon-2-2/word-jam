ABOUT


A palimpsest is a manuscript erased and written over, hiding the previous text. In Palim, create words to reveal the hidden letters underneath.

Connect tiles in any direction to create words of at least 3 letters.

Use all the green tiles to win. Score points for each tile you use: 3 for green, 2 for yellow, and 1 for red.
