HOW TO PLAY


Drag across tiles to make words. You can drag in any direction; words do not need to be in a straight line.

As they are used, tiles are replaced with new ones in different colors and with different letters. Use all the green tiles to win.

Score points by using tiles - 3 points for a green tile, 2 points for a yellow tile, and 1 point for a red tile.

Words must be at least 3 letters long.
