ABOUT


A palimpsest is a piece of writing erased and written over. In Palim, create words to reveal the letters underneath.

Drag across tiles to make words at least 3 letters long. You can drag in any direction; words do not need to be in a straight line.

Score points by using tiles - 3 points for a green tile, 2 points for a yellow tile, and 1 point for a red tile. Use all the green tiles to win.
